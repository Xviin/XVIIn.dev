Claff answers one question: **how much is left to pay this month?**

It started as a subscription tracker. Then I noticed subscriptions are the small part: rent, loans, installments and bills are what really shape the month. So Claff keeps every fixed monthly payment in one place.

## What it does

- **One number at the top.** What's left to pay this month, with a bar showing how much is already paid.
- **Late payments come first.** Anything overdue moves to the top, so nothing slips.
- **The month as a calendar.** Every payment sits on its day.
- **Bills that change.** Electricity, gas and water are marked with ~ as estimates.
- **Installments.** Loans and phone installments show where you are, like 7/24.
- **Widgets.** The month, the next payments and what's late, right on the home screen.

## How it's made

Designed and built by me with AI, from the first sketch to the App Store. Native iPhone app in SwiftUI, with WidgetKit widgets.

Coming to the App Store in November 2026.
